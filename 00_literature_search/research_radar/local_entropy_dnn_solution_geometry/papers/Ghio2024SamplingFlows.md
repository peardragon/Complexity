# Sampling with flows, diffusion, and autoregressive neural networks from a spin-glass perspective

- bibkey: [@Ghio2024SamplingFlows]
- authors: Davide Ghio, Yatin Dandi, Florent Krzakala, Lenka Zdeborova
- year: 2024
- venue: Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences
- doi: 10.1073/pnas.2311810121
- url: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2311810121

## Summary

The paper compares flows, diffusion, and autoregressive neural-network samplers against traditional methods such as MCMC and Langevin dynamics from a spin-glass perspective.

## Method

Theoretical and empirical comparison of modern generative samplers on distributions with known structure.

## Dataset

Spin-glass-inspired target distributions and controlled sampling problems.

## Key Finding

Modern generative samplers have strengths and limitations that can be analyzed through statistical-physics sampling benchmarks.

## Limitation

The target is sampler performance for known distributions, not DNN parameter-space local entropy.
