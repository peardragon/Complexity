# Star-Shaped Space of Solutions of the Spherical Negative Perceptron

- bibkey: [@Annesi2023StarShapedSpace]
- authors: Brandon Livio Annesi, Clarissa Lauditi, Carlo Lucibello, Enrico M. Malatesta, Gabriele Perugini, Fabrizio Pittorino, Luca Saglietti
- year: 2023
- venue: Physical Review Letters
- doi: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.131.227301
- url: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.131.227301

## Summary

The paper studies the geometry of the spherical negative perceptron solution space and argues for a large star-shaped/geodesically simple component in the overparameterized regime.

## Method

Analytical computation of energy barriers in a simplex whose vertices are equilibrium-sampled configurations.

## Dataset

Spherical negative perceptron model.

## Key Finding

Solution manifolds in overparameterized perceptron models can have simple connected structures rather than isolated minima.

## Limitation

The result is model-theoretic and should not be directly equated with empirical DNN parameter-space geometry.

## Relation To My Work
