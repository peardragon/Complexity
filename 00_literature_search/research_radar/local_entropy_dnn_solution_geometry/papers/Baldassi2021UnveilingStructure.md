## Summary

wide flat minima가 high-margin solutions 주변의 extensive structures로 나타난다고 분석했다.

## Method

Analytical/numerical statistical-physics analysis.

## Dataset

Neural-network models with random patterns.

## Key Finding

rare high-margin minima are surrounded by many lower-margin solutions over long distances.

## Limitation

task-specific empirical DNN reference families and estimator QC are outside scope.
