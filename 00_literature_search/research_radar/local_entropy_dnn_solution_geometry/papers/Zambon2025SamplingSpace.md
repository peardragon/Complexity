# Sampling the space of solutions of an artificial neural network

- bibkey: [@Zambon2025SamplingSpace]
- authors: Alessandro Zambon, Enrico M. Malatesta, Guido Tiana, Riccardo Zecchina
- year: 2025
- venue: Physical Review E
- doi: 10.1103/qs48-jzyq
- arxiv: 2503.08266
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08266

## Summary

Hybrid Monte Carlo, ratchet-based connectivity probing, and coupled replica simulations are used to explore low-loss solution space in one-hidden-layer neural networks. The paper reports a spiky low-energy topology near interpolation and a flatter extended structure in the overparameterized regime.
