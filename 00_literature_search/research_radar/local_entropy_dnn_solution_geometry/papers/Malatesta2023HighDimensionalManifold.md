# High-dimensional manifold of solutions in neural networks

## Summary

These pedagogic notes review the statistical-mechanics approach to neural-network solution spaces, especially perceptron models, Gardner capacity, SAT/UNSAT transitions, and the geometric arrangement of zero-training-error configurations.

## Method

Review and synthesis of replica/statistical-mechanics methods for perceptron and neural-network solution manifolds.

## Dataset

Theoretical perceptron and related neural-network models.

## Key Finding

The notes position local landscape characterization around solutions as a central statistical-physics question.

## Limitation

This is a conceptual/theoretical source rather than a new empirical DNN measurement.
