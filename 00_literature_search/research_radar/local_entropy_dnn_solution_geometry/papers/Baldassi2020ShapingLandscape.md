## Summary

one/two-layer neural models에서 wide flat minima의 존재와 cross-entropy 학습의 연결을 분석했다.

## Method

Statistical mechanics and numerical study of simple neural-network landscapes.

## Dataset

Random patterns and real-data numerical checks.

## Key Finding

wide flat minima coexist with narrower minima and correlate with robustness/generalization.

## Limitation
