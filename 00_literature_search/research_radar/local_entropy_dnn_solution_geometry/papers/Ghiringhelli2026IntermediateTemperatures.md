# Sampling at intermediate temperatures is optimal for training large language models in protein structure prediction

- bibkey: [@Ghiringhelli2026IntermediateTemperatures]

- year: 2026
- venue: arXiv
- doi: 10.48550/arXiv.2603.29529
- arxiv: 2603.29529

## Summary

The paper analyzes transformer parameter spaces for protein sequence models with a statistical-mechanics and Langevin-temperature framing.

## Method

Langevin sampling across temperatures to characterize low-loss manifolds in transformer/protein sequence models.

## Dataset

Protein sequence data and transformer-based protein structure prediction context.

## Key Finding

Intermediate temperatures can be associated with favorable learning properties in the studied transformer setting.

## Limitation

It is an application/domain-specific transformer study and should not be generalized directly to MNIST or small DNN rule-family experiments.
